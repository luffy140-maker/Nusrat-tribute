The Legend

A voice that changed the world of Qawwali.

Ustad Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan was one of the greatest Qawwali singers of the modern era. His extraordinary voice, improvisation and spiritual intensity introduced generations of listeners around the world to the power of South Asian Sufi music.

His recordings continue to inspire musicians and listeners across Pakistan, India and far beyond.

This website is a fan-made tribute created to celebrate his music, legacy and contribution to the world of Qawwali.
